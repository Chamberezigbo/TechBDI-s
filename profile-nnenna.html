Team Profile

Nnenna Ogbonna

Founder & Executive Director

Driving inclusive innovation and tech education for underrepresented communities.

Nnenna Ogbonna is the Founder and Executive Director of the Technology Beyond Disability Initiative (TechBDI). With a deep passion for equity and accessibility, she leads the organization's mission to bridge the digital divide for Deaf girls, the girl child, women in underrepresented communities, and persons with disabilities (PWDs) across Nigeria.

Under her leadership, TechBDI has successfully launched programs like the Inclusive Digital Skills Lab (IDSL) and mentored teams such as the STRIM Girls, who became the first all-Deaf team from Nigeria to qualify for the Technovation World Summit.

She is a strong advocate for ensuring that technology serves humanity, and she works tirelessly with partners like the Maryam Babangida National Centre, SEB, and DWAI to create sustainable, systemic change.
