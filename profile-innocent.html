Team Profile

Innocent Ubadire Okoro

Sign Language Tutor | Interpreter | Technology Mentor

Promoting inclusion and empowering underserved communities through communication and technology.

Innocent Ubadire Okoro is a versatile professional with experience in sign language instruction and interpretation, technology mentorship, virtual assistance, freelance writing, and solar PV. He is passionate about inclusion, accessibility, and empowering underserved communities.

As a sign language tutor and interpreter, Innocent promotes communication and inclusion for Deaf individuals. He is also a mentor to the STRIM Girls from GSS Kuje, a team of five Deaf girls who emerged as finalists in the 2025/2026 Technovation Girls Challenge.

Through his mentorship, Innocent supports their participation in technology and innovation, championing greater opportunities for Deaf girls and other underserved young people.

Driven by integrity, respect for diversity, and continuous learning, Innocent is committed to using his skills to empower others and create meaningful community impact.
