Chidinma Ogueri

Product Designer | Creative Designer | Poet | Technology Educator

Creating meaningful design experiences through creativity, technology, and mentorship.

Chidinma Ogueri is a technology-driven creative professional with a background in Computer Science and a passion for meaningful design, creative expression, and accessible technology.

Her work spans UI/UX and product design, visual communication, poetry, creative writing, and digital innovation. She combines creativity, design thinking, and technology to develop user-focused solutions while using words to explore faith, emotions, ideas, and the human experience.

She serves as the Admin and Communications Officer at Technology Beyond Disability Initiative (TechBDI), contributing to administrative coordination, communications, creative design, and initiatives that equip girls, particularly girls with disabilities, with digital skills and opportunities to innovate. She has also mentored girls through the Technovation Girls programme, supporting their journey from ideas to solutions for real-world challenges.

Chidinma is passionate about helping girls and young people discover their strengths, find their path, and gain the confidence to pursue their ideas and aspirations. Through her work in design, technology, and creative expression, she aspires to build meaningful digital experiences, inspire others, and contribute to a world where more people have the opportunity to learn, create, and thrive.
