Okoroafor Alexander

Programs Manager & Special Needs Educator

Designing inclusive educational frameworks for Deaf learners.

Okoroafor Alexander is the Programs Manager and Special Needs Educator at TechBDI. He plays a pivotal role in designing and executing inclusive educational frameworks that cater to the unique needs of Deaf learners and students with disabilities. With a background in special education, Alexander ensures that TechBDI's curriculum is accessible, engaging, and transformative, empowering students to thrive in the digital economy.
