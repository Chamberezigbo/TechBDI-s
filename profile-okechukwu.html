Team Profile

Okechukwu Umezurike

Creative Technologist | Graphic Designer | Frontend Practitioner

Helping businesses, individuals, and young innovators build practical technology skills.

Okechukwu Umezurike is a creative technology enthusiast, graphic designer, and frontend development practitioner with a passion for digital skills education. Through his brand, OJ Elite Skillz, he supports businesses and individuals with graphic design, web development, and practical technology training.

As a mentor with TechBDI under the Technovation Girls Challenge, he is committed to guiding young girls in exploring technology, developing creative solutions to real-world problems, and building confidence in their ability to innovate.
