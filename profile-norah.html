Team Profile

Norah Obinna-Ukoha

Young Tech Innovator | Technovation Girls Mentor | Aspiring Entrepreneur

Exploring how curiosity, critical thinking, and entrepreneurship can solve real problems.

Norah Obinna-Ukoha is a young aspiring tech innovator, mentor, and entrepreneur driven by curiosity, creativity, and a desire to solve problems that matter. She is interested in the intersection of technology, critical thinking, and entrepreneurship, with a focus on developing practical solutions to real-world challenges.

Norah looks beyond the obvious. She asks why problems persist, whether proposed solutions genuinely meet people's needs, and how existing ideas can be improved or reimagined. For her, innovation means understanding a problem deeply enough to build something useful, relevant, and sustainable.

As a Student Ambassador, Norah supports young girls as they explore technology and develop app concepts addressing problems in their societies. Her mentorship emphasizes meaningful questions, independent thinking, and the confidence to turn ideas into practical solutions.

Beyond mentoring, Norah is exploring digital innovation, creative design, and entrepreneurship. She believes young people do not have to wait until they have mastered everything before they begin building, experimenting, and learning through experience.
